The LRS (Learning Record Store) platform is a centralized system for tracking and analyzing student learning data across multiple educational contexts. It stores xAPI statements and provides analytics dashboards.
Navigate to the Students section from the main menu, click the "Add Student" button, and fill in the required fields: name, email, and group assignment. Optional fields include role, status, and progress tracking.
The platform offers progress tracking per student, group-level comparisons, completion rates, time-on-task metrics, and custom report generation. All data can be exported to CSV or PDF formats.
