# d1-002 — Economics of cheap all-in-one AI resellers

## Findings

- **Metered points on thin margin is the only "honest" aggregation model.** Poe ($19.99/mo) is really a compute-points budget: light models ~10–20 pts/msg, frontier models hundreds–thousands; add-on points $30/1M; heavy single-model users are cheaper going direct. Poe protects margin by **resizing the meter, not the sticker**: free tier cut ~90% (~3,000→300 pts/day, Mar 2026, undisclosed) and Premium 1M→660K pts (Jul 2026 = stealth ~51% price-per-point rise, price unchanged). [source: https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/poe] — verified
- **"Unlimited" at ~£10–20 always means a hidden cost-based cap of roughly 5x sticker.** Merlin AI Pro ($19/mo, "unlimited fair access") internally meters ~$16/day / ~$100/month of model cost, then pauses the account ("exceeded fair usage"); the cap **scales down if you paid a promo price**, and top-up credits are the upsell. Generic rule: "only ChatGPT Pro ($200/mo) is truly unlimited; every other 'unlimited' plan has undisclosed caps." [source: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/merlin-ai-pricing (quoting merlin-ai.canny.io); https://aisubscriptioncomparison.com/features/hidden-costs/] — verified
- **Auto-downgrade & silent substitution are mainstream levers.** When limits hit, providers "switch to a less capable model (ChatGPT), block messages (Claude), or reduce priority (Gemini)" rather than bill overage; OpenAI changed GPT-5.6 Sol behavior via verbosity/effort tuning (Aug 2026) and Anthropic's Apr 2026 system-prompt change degraded coding under the same model name. [source: https://aisubscriptioncomparison.com/features/hidden-costs/; https://www.milwaukeeindependent.com/explainers/ai-users-report-degraded-quality-tech-giants-push-reduced-reasoning-models-save-costs/] — verified
- **Grey-market supply exists at 70–98% off list and is exactly the shape a £9.99 "everything" plan needs.** Pooled Claude Max 5x accounts resold as pseudo-API; `newapi`/`sub2api` one-API proxy panels (supply chain: 卡商→account pools→one-api→buyers, per the Vectoral "token brokers" investigation, tens of $M volume, 30–80% discounts); stolen cards funding Max seats; Codex free-trial harvesting cited at 1–5% of API cost. Brokers hand you **a proxy endpoint, not a key** — model substitution is undetectable and marginally incentivized ("how often are tokens cut with other cheap models?" — Kamradt). [source: https://explainx.ai/blog/ai-token-black-market-claude-resellers-distillation-2026; https://reptile.haus/journal/ai-credit-resale-economy-token-brokers-risk-2026/] — reported (secondary, citing Reuters/CNBC/HN)
- **Providers are actively at war with this**: OpenAI Services Agreement bars reselling/leasing access; OpenAI's Codex lead confirmed limit complaints traced to sub2api pooling (Aug 2026); Anthropic added KYC ID checks at Pro/Max checkout (Jul 2026) after a Senate letter alleging 25,000 fraudulent accounts / 28.8M Claude exchanges by Alibaba-affiliated distillation operators. [source: https://reptile.haus/journal/ai-credit-resale-economy-token-brokers-risk-2026/; https://explainx.ai/blog/ai-token-black-market-claude-resellers-distillation-2026 (citing Reuters/CNBC); https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10471989-openai-account-sharing-policy] — verified (OpenAI policy) / reported (letter, KYC)
- **Credits arbitrage is a real secondary market.** Marketplaces resell unused startup/accelerator credits at 30–60% off (one claims $20M traded) — "gift-card exchange" diligence level; supply exists because program credits expire unused. [source: https://reptile.haus/journal/ai-credit-resale-economy-token-brokers-risk-2026/; https://www.getaiperks.com/en/ai/buy-sell-ai-credits] — reported (second is promotional)
- **Data/trace harvesting as revenue.** Free tiers feed training (Gemini API free: "Used to improve our products: Yes" vs paid "No"; Meta AI trains on all chats); grey resellers additionally sell prompts + reasoning traces to labs for distillation — user traffic becomes an asset class. [source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing; https://explainx.ai/blog/ai-token-black-market-claude-resellers-distillation-2026] — verified (Google) / reported (trace resale)
- **Loss-leader subsidies are explicit.** Provider consumer subscriptions are openly subsidized vs API ("that access is subsidized" — reselling it is what ToS bans); aggregators run VC-subsidized growth (Poe = Quora/a16z, revenue ~$30M→$65M 2024→2026); the cheap-inference cycle is "unwinding" as providers reprice. [source: https://manifest.build/blog/banned-from-chatgpt-subscriptions/; https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/poe; https://reptile.haus/journal/ai-credit-resale-economy-token-brokers-risk-2026/] — reported
- **Bait/dark-pattern variant is common at the cheap end.** use.ai (25+ models, one subscription; 30K Trustpilot reviews, 4/5): recurring complaints of £0.50 trials rolling into £29.99 charges, cancellation friction, "limited token allowances" — with heavy reactive refunding by the company. [source: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/use.ai] — verified (fetched reviews)
- **Order-of-magnitude math vs aisure's £9.99 (≈$13.50).** List API (per 1M tok, verified 2026-09): Claude Opus 5 $5/$25, Sonnet 5 $2/$10; GPT-5.6 Sol $4/$20 (Luna $0.20/$1.20); Gemini 3.x Pro $1.35/$6.75 (promo). A heavy text user (20M in / 2M out/month) costs $60 (Sonnet 5) to $150 (Opus 5); add 30 ten-second Sora-2 clips (~$0.10/s ≈ $30) and a few hundred images (cents each). **Realistic heavy month ≈ $70–300+ at list vs £9.99 revenue** — a 5–20x gap, and break-even needs per-user cost <$12 after Stripe fees. Merlin's own economics concede ~5x sticker ($100 cost at $19) *with* a published-ish cap; aisure offers half that sticker with "no daily caps" and no fair-use clause (d1-001), so margin defense must be invisible: server-side throttling (its free tier already proves the `free_daily_limit` lever exists), cheaper substitute/routed models under premium labels, priority-queue degradation, ads+gamification revenue, or simple loss-leader growth. [source: pricing via https://platform.openai.com/docs/pricing + https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/about-claude/pricing + https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing; aisure claims via d1-001] — verified (prices), inference (gap)
- **Batch/cache discounts don't close the gap.** All three providers give 50% Batch API discounts + prompt caching — cuts the heavy-user floor to ~$30–75/month, still 2–5x £9.99 for a genuinely heavy user, and batch is unusable for interactive chat. [source: same pricing pages] — verified

## Tensions

- The only proven sustainable models (Poe's meter, Merlin's hidden $-cost cap, providers' own message caps) all *meter aggressively*; aisure's published promise ("no daily caps on any tool", no fair-use clause) is inconsistent with every comparator at its price point — either unpublished throttles exist or the unit economics don't close.
- Aggregator labels ("GPT-5.6", "Gemini 3 Pro") are unverifiable through any proxy — no public audit exists; even first-party providers tune capability under a stable name.
- Grey-market discounts (70–98%) are large enough that a small operator could undercut list price *legitimately-seeming* while running pooled/stolen supply — and the enforcement risk (mid-service termination) lands on users, not the reseller.
