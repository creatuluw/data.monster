Why is aisure.uk so cheap?

AISure (aisure.uk) sells “every AI tool, one platform” — GPT‑5, GPT‑5.6, Gemini 3 Pro, Claude — with a free tier and Pro at £9.99/mo “unlimited”. It is cheap because the advertised promise cannot cost what it charges: a heavy user of those models costs $70–300+/month at list API prices versus £9.99 (≈$13.50) of revenue, so the gap is closed by invisible server-side throttling, possibly cheaper models behind premium labels, ad/gamification revenue, and a growth-subsidized bet — all under a months-old domain with hidden WHOIS and 0/100 (Scamadviser) / 35/100 (Gridinsoft) trust scores. Red flags abound; proof of fraud does not (the UK company is real, Stripe is merchant of record).

The offer and its fine print

Verified from the site’s own JavaScript bundles (it is a client-rendered Lovable/Supabase SPA, so the served code is the source of truth):

  • Free tier is not “free forever” on the site — that phrase appears only in Facebook/Instagram ads. The site says “Free to Start”, with hard server-side daily caps per tool (free_daily_limit, reset timers) and ads (Google Ads, Meta, TikTok pixels). verified
  • Pro (£9.99/mo, £99/yr via Stripe) “lifts every daily cap and unlocks every premium model” — and no fair-use clause exists anywhere; Terms only say “Features, models and limits may change.” verified
  • Frontier models are named explicitly (GPT-5, GPT-5.6, Gemini 3 Pro, Claude), cheaper than any single frontier subscription. verified
  • Careful legal dressing: UK-GDPR privacy policy, raw-prompt storage opt-in, 14-day no-questions refund, Stripe as seller of record, liability capped at £100. Contracting entity Noahsure Group LTD, 1 Canada Square, Canary Wharf. verified

The economics: how “£9.99 unlimited” can exist

Every sustainable comparator meters aggressively; AISure promises more for less, so the margin defense must be invisible:

  • List-API math: a heavy month of those models = $70–300+; Pro revenue is ≈$13.50. Even 50% batch discounts leave a 2–5× gap. verified
  • Poe ($19.99) is really a compute-point budget (quietly cut ~90% free points, ~51% stealth price-per-point rise); Merlin’s $19 “unlimited” hides a ~$100/month cost cap that pauses your account. “Unlimited” at half that sticker with no published cap matches no viable comparator. verified
  • Model substitution under premium labels is a mainstream margin lever and unauditable through proxy endpoints; AISure’s “priority queue” language and proven per-tool limit machinery are exactly the invisible-throttle pattern. inference
  • Grey-market supply (pooled/resold accounts, trial harvesting) sells frontier access at 70–98% off list at scale — exactly the discount a £9.99 “everything” plan needs. No evidence AISure uses it; the class fits. reported pattern
  • The free tier is revenue-positive, not a loss: ads + /advertise slot sales + affiliate program + paid playbooks + hundreds of programmatic-SEO pages + referral gamification. verified

Trust signals

  • Scamadviser 0/100 “Caution”: domain registered 2026-03-27 (1-year term), hidden WHOIS, flagged Phishing+Suspicious by IPQS; hosted in Germany via Cyber Assets Fzco (UAE-named ISP). verified
  • Gridinsoft 35/100, “avoid entering personal or payment data”. reported
  • Zero organic trust surface: no Trustpilot profile found, no Reddit/Quora threads, no press — only paid Facebook/Instagram ads. verified absence
  • The one independent hands-on review (LinkedIn, Jun 2026): Delete Account didn’t work, public /api/health leaked internal key names, forex/prediction “capital partnership” links, Noahsure email bounced. Reviewer explicitly says “not automatically fraudulent”. verified
  • Counterweight: Noahsure Group Limited (12364489) is real, Active since Dec 2019, at the exact Terms address; Stripe merchant of record; generous refunds. verified

Tensions

Tension

“Unlimited, no caps, no fair use” (offer, verified) vs an economics that requires caps at every comparator (economics, verified). Assessment: unpublished server-side throttling almost certainly exists; the sticker is marketing, the meter is invisible.

Tension

“UK built” marketing vs German hosting, UAE-named ISP, hidden WHOIS, months-old domain (trust, verified). A clean 2019 London company backs a March-2026 anonymous web presence. Assessment: the legal shell is real; the operational footprint is not what the ads imply.

Tension

Trust scores of 0/100 vs no consumer-fraud complaints anywhere. Assessment: plausibly IPQS false-positives reacting to ad-heavy programmatic SEO — flags are signals, not findings of fraud.
